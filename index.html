Our story

Started with a garage roaster and a stubborn sourdough starter.

Nora and Sam Bergstrom opened The Wren in 2017 with twelve seats and one espresso machine that only worked if you talked nicely to it. Seven years on we roast every bean in-house, bake every morning from four a.m., and still name the starter. (It's Gerald. He's doing great.)

Everything on the plate comes from as close as we can get it — flour from Lonesome Stone mill, eggs from Miller Farm down County Road B, maple from Sam's uncle.
